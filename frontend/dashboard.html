Help & Candidate FAQs

Frequently asked questions about AI scoring, proctoring, CV verification, and support options.

How does the AI interview scoring work? ▼
Our system employs a multimodal assessment pipeline: Whisper Speech Recognition transcribes your answers, Sentence Transformers & NLP evaluate technical correctness and relevance against domain benchmarks, and MediaPipe tracks eye contact, confidence, and facial engagement.
Why does it say "Name Mismatch on CV"? ▼
For exam integrity, the candidate's registered name must match the name extracted from the uploaded CV. If there is a typo or maiden name variance, please re-upload a CV containing your official registered name or update your profile name in the Profile section.
Can I retake an interview if I'm dissatisfied with my score? ▼
Yes! You can initiate a new interview session from the "Start Interview" tab. The system selects adaptive questions suited to your target role, and all attempts remain logged in your Interview History.
What are the webcam and microphone requirements? ▼
You need a standard webcam (720p or higher recommended) and a working microphone. Ensure browser permissions are granted when prompted. You can test your devices in the "Start Interview" tab diagnostic before starting.
How does proctoring and face detection work? ▼
The system monitors face presence in real-time. If no face is detected or if multiple faces appear, a proctoring warning is issued. Continued violation will automatically terminate the session to uphold institutional integrity.
How do I test my webcam before the interview? ▼
Navigate to the Start Interview tab and click Test Camera & Microphone. A live webcam preview will appear. Confirm the image is clear, well-lit, and your face is centered. Once verified, click Confirm Working to mark the hardware check as passed.
My microphone is not being detected — what should I do? ▼
Ensure your browser has microphone permission enabled. Click the lock or camera icon in your browser's address bar and allow both camera and microphone access. Then click Retry Camera in the diagnostics panel. If the issue persists, check your operating system's privacy settings under Settings → Privacy → Microphone and ensure your browser is allowed.
What is the difference between Identity Verified and the screening score? ▼
Identity Verification checks whether the name on your uploaded CV matches your registered account name — this is a binary pass/fail check for institutional integrity. The Screening Score (e.g., 27.7%) is a separate NLP-based score measuring how well your CV content matches the selected target role's requirements. A low screening score does not mean identity failure.
